1. What the app does

Compresso re-encodes photos and videos in your device's photo library so they occupy less storage, and can delete items at your instruction. It acts only on items you select. It shows you each item before anything happens to it.

2. Your library is your responsibility

Compression is lossy. A compressed photo or video is a new, smaller file that is visually very close to the original but is not byte-for-byte identical to it. When you choose to replace an original with its compressed copy, the original is moved to your system Recently Deleted album, where iOS keeps it for about 30 days before removing it permanently.

You are responsible for maintaining your own backups. We strongly recommend having iCloud Photos or another backup in place before compressing or deleting anything. We are not able to recover media that iOS has permanently removed.

The app deliberately declines to compress certain capture formats — among them RAW, Portrait, Cinematic, spatial, slow-motion and time-lapse — because re-encoding them would discard data the format carries beyond the plain image or video. Those items are left untouched.
